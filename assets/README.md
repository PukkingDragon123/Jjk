# Character art (drop your fan-art here)

The app loads each sorcerer's art from this folder. It works **without** these
files (you'll see a stencil kanji as a placeholder), but to use your own art,
save the 4 images here with **exactly** these names:

| File                | Character        | Which image you sent |
|---------------------|------------------|----------------------|
| `assets/sukuna.png` | Ryomen Sukuna    | 1st photo            |
| `assets/yuji.png`   | Yuji Itadori     | 2nd photo            |
| `assets/gojo.png`   | Gojo Satoru      | 3rd photo            |
| `assets/megumi.png` | Megumi Fushiguro | 4th photo            |

Tips:
- `.png` is expected. Transparent background looks best on the sticker cards,
  but a plain white background (like the art you sent) also looks great.
- Tall/portrait images work well — they're shown standing on the card.
- After adding the files, just refresh the page; they'll appear on the
  character-select stickers, the in-game HUD badge, and stamped on your
  captured photos.
